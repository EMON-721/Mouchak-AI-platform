# User features — BazarCompare AI

1. Guest home: no sign-in required for basket estimates; type, microphone optional, snap handwritten list, or manual add; one CTA “আজকের বাজার তালিকা”
2. Search market products by Bangla or English aliases using seeded dictionary; disambiguate raw vs cooked, local vs imported, dozen vs piece
3. Shopping list: add quantity/unit, known shop quote or personally observed price; show BDT running total and unit-normalized line items
4. Compare multiple offers from user-entered price quotes, date and market with transparent equal-unit conversion; avoid unknown-date comparisons
5. Location preference: division/district/market only from validated list; “অবস্থান না জানলেও চলবে” guest default
6. Verified price board: show official DAM/TCB only where actually ingested, rights-cleared, dated and attributed; separate from crowdsourced and user private prices
7. History view: date-based basket spend, spending breakdown, repeat basket templates, immutable saved price snapshots
8. Budget planner: user adds budget and essential priorities; affordable basket suggestions with honest constraints and no unverified alternatives
9. Offer unit price comparator: compare 500g vs 1kg pack price, 1 dozen vs 6 pieces, exact formula shown; missing density conversions not fabricated
10. Community reports: users may submit photo or price notes; moderation queue, source flags, independent verification, expiry/staleness scoring
11. Accessibility: Bengali numerals and Latin switch, quick family share export after consent, printable PDF/WhatsApp copyable text, low-bandwidth mode

# Specialized Admin features

1. Market/source CRUD: place identity, location hierarchy, source profile, observed vs published date, freshness policy per commodity
2. Human-assisted DAM/TCB report importer with PDF/CSV columns, user-reviewed source PDF and two-stage publish approval
3. Community quote moderation with duplicate and image evidence handling, trust score trace, spam/false pricing dispute queue
4. Canonical products editor including grades (মিনিকেট/নাজিরশাইল etc only when defined correctly), units, local/imported variants and aliases
5. Price dashboard by source quality and staleness, data corrections and mass rollback; never let marketing team alter official published prices without audit
